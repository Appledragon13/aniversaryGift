One Year

One year
Yet I feel like I know you in every life
Like every version of me before now was just waiting
to rest my head in your lap
and feel your fingers tangled in my hair
as the world counted down to something new.

I still remember that night,
the glow of the TV,
the sound of your family chatting,
the ball dropping,
and you,
kissing me like time had stopped just for us.
It wasn’t all the noise that made my heart race,
it was you.

You with your maple brown eyes,
warm, steady, the kind of safe that doesn’t fade.
You with that laugh
that could pull me out of my darkest storm.
You with that smile
that feels like home every damn time.

And when you look at me,
when I call you my good boy
and you light up like the sun’s finally breaking through the clouds,
I see everything I never thought I’d find.
Not perfection.
Not some fairytale.
But something real.
Something that holds me,
that loves me in the broken spaces,
that helps me breathe again.

Luke,
you are the calm in my chaos,
the warmth in my cold nights,
and the truth I didn’t know I needed.
A year ago, I didn’t know love could feel like this,
so full, so fierce,
so healing.
And if this is only the first year,
then I can’t wait
to see what the rest of forever looks like with you.
